## Overview

PlayBloom Toys links play with creativity, learning and skill development. The social strategy gives parents and educators a reason to follow, engage and buy—combining educational content, real play moments, product demos, social proof and targeted campaigns.

**Platforms**
| Platform | Role |
|----------|------|
| **Instagram** | Main brand showcase & daily contact |
| **Facebook** | Trust, community, retargeting |
| **TikTok** | Discovery via short-form video |

## Key targets (indicative, first 3–6 months)

| Area | KPI | Target |
|------|-----|--------|
| Awareness | Reach | +25% monthly (early phase) |
| Engagement | Engagement rate | ≥4% organic |
| Traffic | Paid CTR | ≥1.5–2.0% |
| Sales | Social conversion rate | 1.5–2.5% after learning phase |
| Efficiency | CPA | -10–15% after 6–8 weeks |
| Revenue | ROAS | ≥2.5 initial |
